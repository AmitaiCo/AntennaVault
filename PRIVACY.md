# AntennaVault privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

AntennaVault is a podcast player based on the open-source app AntennaPod. It has no user accounts, no
advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developers of AntennaVault do not run any server that
receives your data.

## Data stored on your device

AntennaVault stores your subscriptions, episodes, playback progress, downloaded episodes and settings on
your device only.

The guardian code is never stored in readable form. Only a salted hash of it is kept, together with the
unlock and lockout times. This data never leaves the device and is excluded from device backups and
device-to-device transfers.

## Network connections

AntennaVault only connects to:

- **Podcast servers.** To show, refresh and download a podcast, AntennaVault contacts the server that
  hosts it. Like any website, that server can see your IP address and that the request comes from
  AntennaVault.
- **Podcast directories.** When you search for podcasts, your search terms are sent to the directories
  used for the search (Apple Podcasts, Podcast Index and, if you choose it, fyyd). Podcast suggestions
  are loaded from Apple Podcasts' charts for your country.
- **Synchronization, if you set it up.** If you connect a gpodder.net or Nextcloud account, your
  subscription list and listening progress are sent to the server you chose. This is off unless you turn
  it on.

## Android backup

If backup is enabled on your device, Android may back up your list of subscriptions to your Google
account, as described in your device's backup settings. The guardian code and the episode database are
not included.

## Crash reports

If AntennaVault crashes, a report is saved on your device. It is only shared if you choose to send it
yourself from Settings > Report bug.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be asked at https://github.com/AmitaiCo/AntennaVault/issues.
